Privacy Policy

Version of 21 October 2026. This policy describes which data the Humaníadas app handles and where it lives. In short: the app has no accounts and no servers of its own, collects no telemetry and uses no analytics or advertising services. Everything it creates stays on your device, including the crash reports, which only leave it if you decide to send them.

Data stored on the device

To work, the app stores locally:

  • your settings (language, appearance, font size, daily reminder);
  • the daily draws, the unlocked poems, the streak, the hazelnuts and the acorns;
  • the journal notes, the favourites and the reading position;
  • the records of purchases made on Google Play or the App Store (purchase and product identifiers, date), needed to keep access to what you bought.

This data never leaves the device on the app's initiative. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What we do not collect

No account is created; we do not ask for a name, e-mail address, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. The app sends no usage statistics, includes no analytics, advertising or social-network SDKs, and does not track your activity.

Crash reports

When the app meets an unexpected error it writes a report on the device with the error message, the stack trace, the app and content versions, the device model and a few display settings. It never includes poem text or your notes. The reports sit under "Settings › Crash reports", where you can view and delete them; they are only sent if you decide to share them, through the system share sheet, to the destination you choose (for example the support e-mail).

Backups

You can export your data to an encrypted file, optionally protected by a passphrase only you know, and keep it wherever you like through the system share sheet. The app keeps no copy of that file or of the passphrase.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play, which handles your payment details under Google's privacy policy. The app receives from the store only the purchase confirmation (identifier, product, date and the receipt issued by the store) and keeps it on the device to recognise what you bought and to allow restoring purchases.

On iPhone, purchases are processed by the App Store, run by Apple, which handles your payment details under Apple's privacy policy. The app receives from the App Store only the purchase confirmation, on the same terms, and purchases are restored with the same Apple ID through "Restore purchases".

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the chosen time. This needs the system's notification permission and involves no server.

Sharing

When you share a poem, a backup or a crash report, the content is handed to the app you pick in the system share sheet; from there on that app's policies apply.

Children

The app collects no personal data and is not specifically aimed at children. The age rating is assigned on Google Play and the App Store.

Your rights

Since the data lives only on your device, you control it directly: you can view it in the app, export it and delete it (by deleting notes, reports or the app itself). To exercise your GDPR rights or ask questions, contact us.

Changes

This policy may be updated with new versions of the app; the version in force ships with the installed app and carries the date shown at the top.
